*Functional Requirements*

1. *Authentication* (2 pages)
- A login & register pages for teachers with username and password fields.
- Password recovery or reset functionality.

2. *Dashboard Home Screen*
- Display a list of subjects taught by the teacher.
- Navigation to a subject's detailed page.

3. *Subject Management*
- Add, edit, or delete subjects.
- View a subject's structure, including units and lessons.
- Add or remove units for a subject.
- Add or remove lessons within units.

4. *Lesson Management*
- Add lessons to units:
  - Upload video files from the teacher's device.
  - Embed videos via YouTube links.
  - Upload PDF files.
- Edit or delete existing lessons.

5. *Student Management*
- Add students to a subject or unit based on their unique codes and selected packages.
- View a student's profile (if the student is enrolled in at least one subject taught by the teacher).
- Change a student's password.
- Remove a student from a subject.

#### 6. *Packages*
- Associate a student with specific subjects or units depending on their chosen package.

---

### *Non-Functional Requirements*

1. *Performance*
   - Handle concurrent users efficiently.
   - Support large file uploads (videos and PDFs).

2. *Security*
   - Protect sensitive information like passwords and student profiles.
   - Validate file types during uploads to prevent malicious content.

3. *User Interface*
   - Intuitive design for teachers to navigate and manage content easily.
   - Mobile and tablet compatibility for accessibility.

4. *Scalability*
   - Allow for growth in the number of teachers, subjects, and students without performance degradation.

5. *Data Integrity*
   - Ensure that student codes and associated packages are unique and stored reliably.
   - Prevent accidental deletion of essential content.
